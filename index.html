Sentry

An architecture first, a vehicle second.

Platform independent

Mission logic, data structures and operator workflow move between carriers. Platform differences stay in an adapter layer.

Local first

Each node senses, estimates state and maps at the edge. Weak links reduce coordination without stopping basic operation.

Team of machines

One operator supervises a mixed group through a single view instead of steering each machine by hand.

Built on open interfaces

Documented adapters let radios, sensors, positioning sources and compute modules be swapped without redesigning the system.

Arbitration

Threshold Arbitration Framework.

  • A separate layer.It sits above autonomy and decides whether an action proceeds, pauses or goes to a person.
  • Never a targeting engine.It arbitrates; it does not generate lethal action. Our proposed scope excludes autonomous weapon employment.
  • Authority stays human.Approval points sit outside the AI decision loop, with audit logs and explicit authorization states.
  • Original doctrine.Details are shared with partners under agreement.
